The Game

Every block is a beginning

Landtale Online drops you into a living, procedurally-crafted realm where handmade dungeons, roaming factions and seasonal weather collide. Adventure alone, or bring a whole server with you.

⚔️

Fluid Combat

Block, parry, dodge-roll and combo. Melee, ranged and elemental magic weave into a system that rewards timing over gear score.

🏰

Deep Building

Thousands of blocks, furniture, redstone-like circuitry and terrain sculpting tools. Build a hut, a harbour or an entire kingdom.

🧭

Endless Exploration

Handcrafted ruins seeded across generated continents. Every cave, shipwreck and sky island hides something worth the trip.

Worlds

Seven zones. One continent.

Zone 1

Emberwood

Glowing groves, moss-eaten ruins and the Trickster's warrens beneath.
Zone 3

Sunken Sands

Canyon mesas over a buried dynasty. Bring water, and better armour.
Zone 5

Hollowfrost

Aurora skies, ice-locked caverns and outposts that trade in secrets.
